There's no Billing Type field to set anymore. If you think a repair is under warranty, you flag it on the ticket instead, and the office decides whether it's covered.

## When to flag

- The machine was bought recently and the problem sounds like it should still be under manufacturer warranty.
- A part on the machine was replaced recently and it's failed again.
- Anything else that makes you think the manufacturer or vendor, not the customer, should be covering this repair.

You can flag it any time from when you're assigned the ticket through when you complete it. You don't need to wait until the job is finished.

## How to flag it

1. Open the service ticket and find the **Warranty** box.
2. Tap **Flag for warranty review**.
3. In the note, write what makes you think it's covered: the serial number, the purchase date, or the part that was recently replaced.
4. Submit the flag.

## What happens next

The office verifies coverage: whether the labor is covered, which parts are covered, and who the vendor is. You keep working the ticket normally either way.

Either way, you complete the ticket at full price, same as any other job: log your real hours and parts. Coverage doesn't change what you write up. Once the office verifies it, CallBoard handles netting the covered amount off what the customer is actually billed, and the office handles the vendor credit from there.

## Tips

- Give the office something to work with. A serial number or purchase date gets a faster verdict than "might be warranty."
- If you flag the wrong ticket or change your mind, you (or the office) can remove the flag from the ticket while it's still pending.
